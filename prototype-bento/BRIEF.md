## PROJECT
The official bilingual (Arabic-first / English) website of Sana Al-Awael
Contracting Company (SACC, شركة سنا الأوائل للمقاولات), a Saudi
infrastructure contractor founded in 2007 and headquartered in Al Madinah
Al Munawwarah. Its single most important job: give government and private
clients verifiable proof of qualification (approvals, ISO certificates,
delivered projects, owned fleet, leadership) and turn it into quote
requests, while ranking well when people search for the company or for
contractors in the Kingdom.

## AUDIENCE
Two primary audiences, weighted equally:
- Government and semi-government clients: procurement officers and
  project engineers at entities such as the National Water Company, the
  Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture, the Ministry of
  Transport, the Saudi Electricity Company and Al Madinah Municipality.
  They arrive during prequalification or tender shortlisting. They are
  skeptical of unverified claims, and are convinced only by named
  approvals, ISO certificates, comparable delivered projects, fleet
  capacity and leadership.
- Private developers and main contractors: they need a specialist
  subcontractor for a package, or equipment rental from the fleet. They
  want the same proof plus a fast route to a quote.

Secondary audience: engineers and technical staff looking for work. They
use the Careers page and its application form with a CV upload.

## TONE & REGISTER
Formal, orderly, modern, institutional, rooted in infrastructure work.
It speaks to procurement officers, not consumers: plain, confident and
factual, with every claim tied to a certificate, a year, a count or a
named client. Calm and airy rather than loud. Never showy, never a
generic template, never playful. The Arabic must read as polished,
formal Arabic, not translated text.

## REFERENCES
Sirion's website (desktop and mobile screenshots supplied by the owner).
Take its character, not its content or brand: the result must be an
original SACC site.

Take:
- Palette spirit: a deep blue-green page background, feature cards
  alternating between a pale lavender tint and a darker teal, and one
  saturated accent reserved for the single primary action. The exact
  colours are in COLOR PALETTE below.
- Type: large, light-weight sans-serif headings in sentence case, set at
  moderate sizes. Calm and airy, never heavy or shouting.
- Hero: headline on one side, short supporting line and the action on
  the other, then a wide band of real field photographs below.
- Feature grid: a bento layout of rounded cards in mixed widths. Each
  card holds a real photo framed by thin line-drawn connectors and
  nodes, read as engineering or network diagrams (pipes, roads, grids).
  Use it for the nine disciplines.
- Faint background line and grid motifs between sections, reading as
  site plans or utility network lines.
- A row of portrait cards with the photo on top and the title below.
  Use it for leadership.
- A recognition band of badges. Use it for the ISO certificates and the
  government approvals.
- A closing band that splits text and photo.
- A mobile version that stacks cleanly into a single column.

Do NOT take (owner rules override the reference):
- Client testimonials. None exist and none may be invented. The
  approvals and ISO band takes their place.
- A large multi-column sitemap footer. Keep the footer compact, with no
  repeat of the nav.
- Repeated call-to-action buttons. There is ONE quote action only.
- Sirion's text, illustrations, logos or brand assets.

The colours must read saturated and clear (owner requirement):
- Apply them at full strength as solid fills.
- No transparency washes and no greyed-out tints.
- No muddy overlays: no gradients, no glass or blur.
- Photographs stay in natural full colour: no greyscale, no
  desaturation, no heavy dark scrims over them.

- Calm, precise and physical. Never showy, never a carnival.
- The hero photo stays fixed while the page scrolls over it until it
  disappears.
- Gentle fade-up reveals as sections enter the viewport.

  once, softly.

  action is used.

- Animation must never slow access to information.

## CONSTRAINTS
Language and layout:
- Arabic at the root and English under /en, at full parity.
- RTL built natively with CSS logical properties.
- Correct lang and dir on the document.

Owner rules:
- No repetition anywhere: every fact in one place only.
- ONE quote action ("اطلب عرض سعر" / "Request a quote"). It is never
  repeated in every section.
- No "انضم إلينا" tab in the nav.
- Compact footer that does not repeat the nav.
- Moderate text sizes. The owner found large display text too big.
- Real content only; never invent facts.

Stack:
- React + Vite frontend.
- Django + PostgreSQL backend that owns the content (editable in the
  admin) and stores form submissions.
- Quote requests: POST /api/v1/leads/quote-requests/
- Applications: POST /api/v1/careers/applications/
- Staff are notified at info@saccgroup.net.

SEO:
- Real path URLs, prerendered HTML.
- hreflang, sitemap.
- JSON-LD (Organization / LocalBusiness).
- 301 redirects from the old /ar links.

Accessibility:
- WCAG 2.2 level AA.
- Full keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
- Alt text on every photo.
- Labelled form fields.

Security:
- The repository is public: no secrets, .env files or uploaded CVs in git.
