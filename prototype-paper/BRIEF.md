## PROJECT
The official bilingual (Arabic-first / English) website of Sana Al-Awael
Contracting Company (SACC, شركة سنا الأوائل للمقاولات), a Saudi
infrastructure contractor founded in 2007 and based in Al Madinah Al
Munawwarah. Its main job: give government and private clients verifiable
proof of qualification (approvals, ISO, delivered projects, owned fleet,
leadership) and turn that into contact from qualified clients, while
ranking well when people search for the company or for contractors in the
Kingdom.

This is a visual and structural redesign of the existing site. Content,
URLs, forms and the backend stay as they are; the look, the layout and
the home-page composition change to follow the new reference below.

## AUDIENCE
- Government and semi-government clients (equal priority): procurement
  officers and project engineers at the National Water Company, the
  Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture, the Ministry of
  Transport, the Saudi Electricity Company and Al Madinah Municipality.
  They arrive during prequalification or tender shortlisting. They doubt
  unverified claims, and are convinced only by named approvals, ISO
  certificates, comparable delivered projects, fleet capacity and
  leadership.
- Private developers and main contractors (equal priority): they need a
  specialist subcontractor or equipment rental. They want the same proof
  plus a fast route to contact.
- Secondary: engineers and technical staff looking for work (Careers page
  with CV upload).

## TONE & REGISTER
Formal, orderly, modern, institutional, B2B. It speaks to procurement
officers, not consumers: plain, confident and factual, with every claim
tied to a certificate, a year, a count or a named client. Calm and clear,
with generous empty space around every block. Never showy, never a
generic template, never playful.

## REFERENCES
The attached reference website (two screenshots: desktop and mobile).
Take its character, layout system and rhythm, not its content or brand:
the result must be an original SACC site. Nothing of the reference's
text, logos, product screens or illustrations may be copied.

### Overall character to take
- A LIGHT site. Warm off-white paper background, dark slate text, a
  great deal of white space, very few colours, and no decoration that
  does not carry information.
- An editorial, quiet page: each section is one idea, introduced by a
  small label, a heading and a short paragraph, then one strong visual
  block. Sections are separated by space alone, not by lines or boxes.
- Photographs do the emotional work; type and cards stay restrained.

Contrast rules (measured, binding):
- Body text is `#364044` (9.9:1 on paper) or `#545A5B` (6.5:1 on paper).
- `#7B7C79` and `#857C6D` reach only about 3.8:1 on paper, so they are
  allowed for large text only. `#949690` (2.8:1) is never used for text.
- On `#C9C8C3` use `#364044` (6.4:1) for all text.
- On `#A99E8F` and `#839FA3` use `#364044` for headings and large text
  only (about 4:1 and 3.8:1). Put longer text on a paper or white card
  placed on the band, not on the band itself.
- White cards floating on photographs use pure paper `#F7F6F3`.
- The SACC logo is never recoloured. Check that it sits well on paper
  and on the hero photograph; if it does not read on the photograph, put
  the header on a paper bar instead of over the photograph.

### Type
- Large, LIGHT-weight headings in sentence case (weight 300–400), tight
  line height, calm and airy. Never bold display type, never all caps
  for headings.
- Two-tone headings: the first line in ink `#364044`, the continuation
  in muted `#7B7C79`. Example pattern: "Nine disciplines / under one
  contractor" (use the real section titles from the content).
- A small label above each section heading (12–13px, wide letter
  spacing, `#545A5B`). In Arabic the label is not letter-spaced.
- Body text 16–17px, line length 60–70 characters.
- Moderate sizes throughout (owner rule): hero heading about 40–64px on
  desktop and 30–36px on phones; section headings about 28–40px.
- Arabic: a clean, modern, light-capable Arabic face (IBM Plex Sans
  Arabic at 300/400/500 is already in the project). Latin: a matching
  light grotesque (Onest is already in the project). The reference's
  Latin headings have a soft, slightly humanist look; match that feeling
  in both scripts rather than its exact font.
- Numerals in figures, dates and phone numbers stay left-to-right and
  tabular.

### Layout system
- One centred content column, about 1200–1280px wide, with wide side
  margins. On phones a 16–20px gutter.
- Section rhythm: a very tall gap between sections (about 120–160px on
  desktop, 72–96px on phones).
- Section header pattern, used everywhere: label and two-tone heading on
  the start side; a short paragraph on the end side, aligned to the top
  of the heading. On phones they stack.
- Corners: large photo panels and bands have a small radius (about
  8–12px); cards about 6–8px; the action button is a small rounded
  rectangle, not a pill.
- No shadows except one soft shadow on white cards that float over
  photographs. No gradients except the natural blur of a photograph.
- Hairlines `#C9C8C3`, 1px, for list rows only.

## OWNER DECISIONS AFTER APPROVAL
These came after the brief was approved and override it where they differ.

- The single action is "تواصل معنا" / "Contact us".
- Arabic type must read as formal. Of five faces shown, the owner accepted
  Noto Naskh Arabic or Almarai.
- The result must match the reference site's look and its level of finish
  (the owner attached a close-up of its phone hero): serif headings, a light
  header bar, pale card plates, and the hero action in a translucent frame.

- After seeing the first build: make the components consistent and matching
  the reference, then "the components are too big; make them finer and
  smaller so the page is simple and clear; match the reference".

How the prototype applies them:
- Headings: Newsreader Light (Latin) with Noto Naskh Arabic. Body and
  interface: Onest (Latin) with Almarai (Arabic). This replaces the "light
  grotesque / IBM Plex Sans Arabic" line in the Type section.
- Scale: the reference's fine scale. Body 15–15.5px, card text 13.5px,
  labels 11.5px, buttons 13.5–14.5px; section headings 25–40px on two lines
  (ink, then muted).
- Header: a paper bar. The official lockup's wordmark is white and may not be
  recoloured, so the bar shows the official circular mark with the company's
  name as text, and the full lockup sits on the slate footer.
- Cards: pale warm plates (a tint of #A99E8F over the paper) with near-square
  corners, a small serif title, small grey text and a firm small foot line,
  without a hairline.
- Bands: pale tints of #A99E8F (approvals) and #839FA3 (office) instead of
  the solid colours, as in the reference; this also lets small text sit on
  them at full contrast.
- Section links ("All projects", "Approvals in full") are the reference's
  small dark buttons. They are navigation; the contact action stays single.
- Projects: two wide photograph cards per row, as in the reference, instead
  of 3 × 2.
- On narrow screens the ISO standards are a three-row list, not three plates.
- Then: "make the components smaller: the boxes, and of course the type
  inside them". The nine disciplines are five narrow boxes to a row, as in
  the reference, with a tenth dark box linking to all disciplines; two to a
  row on phones, where the capabilities line is left to the discipline's
  page. Projects are three to a row. ISO plates are short. Box titles are
  15.5px and box text 12–12.5px.
- Then: "background colour white; box colour light beige". The page ground is
  #FFFFFF and the boxes #F3F0E7, which is the reference's own card colour
  (243,240,230 sampled from the owner's screenshot). The approvals band is the
  reference's cream (#EAE7D3). These three sit outside the ten-colour list by
  the owner's instruction; text, lines, buttons and the dark surfaces still
  come from it.
- Inner pages keep one large heading; their sections use a smaller step.
- The owner then asked for no attempt to persuade clients or candidates.
  A rewrite of the promotional copy as plain statements was tried and
  rejected: "restore the original texts". The copy stays as the content
  bundle has it.

  columns instead of one dotted line.
- Then, with the same design kept as it is: polish its interaction and motion.
  Pressable things answer a press (a slight scale); hover effects apply to
  pointer devices only; only transform and opacity move; interface changes
  stay under 300ms on a strong ease-out curve; the home page's bar changes
  colour by transition instead of sliding in; sections arrive once as they
  come into view, in a short stagger; the hero's name, sentence, action and
  figures arrive in turn on first sight; cards returning from a filter and a
  tab's panel fade in; reduced motion keeps colour and opacity changes and
  drops movement.
- Buttons and inputs look small but keep a 44px hit area on touch screens.
