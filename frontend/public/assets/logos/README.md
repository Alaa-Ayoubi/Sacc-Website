# Logos of the approving bodies

The approving bodies' official logos, re-encoded from the design prototype's
copies (removed from `main`, kept in the repository's history) as lossless WebP (same pixels, about half the weight). Each is named by its key and
listed, with its display size, in `BODIES` in src/lib/site.js.

| Key | Body |
|---|---|
| `nwc` | National Water Company |
| `sca` | Saudi Contractors Authority |
| `sec` | Saudi Electricity Company |
| `aramco` | Saudi Aramco |
| `maaden` | Ma'aden |
| `rcjy` | Royal Commission for Jubail and Yanbu |
| `momah` | Ministry of Municipalities and Housing |

Use only the files the bodies publish; never redraw or recolour a logo.

## Provenance

`nwc`, `sca`, `sec`, `aramco` and `maaden` were supplied by the owner on
2026-11-23 as screenshots or exported images. Only their flat backgrounds were
made transparent (NWC on light grey, SEC on white) and their margins trimmed;
no logo pixel was changed. The owner then replaced the Aramco and Ma'aden files
(first supplied in their white versions for dark grounds) with their positive
versions, so every plate is white; Ma'aden's white background was made
transparent the same way.

`rcjy` and `momah` were supplied by the owner on 2026-11-23 on white
backgrounds, made transparent the same way. The ministry's logo carries its
current name, «وزارة البلديات والإسكان» / Ministry of Municipalities and
Housing, which the site uses as the logo's text alternative.
